Cafes La Caribeña

OWN ELABORATION

Coffee producers

Robustiano Carrillo Chacón built our famous “La Caribeña” coffee roastery in the town of Macael (Almería), together with his family, in 1994.

Cafés La Caribeña was the first company in the province of Almería to leave the grinder and coffee machine on deposit in a restaurant and to establish a practical after-sales service for its services.

We offer the best after-sales service in the province of Almería, with a barista who explains how to make the best coffees, cappuccino, espresso or the perfect Cortado. We also have a classroom at our headquarters where our experienced baristas give courses and conferences about this interesting world of coffee.
